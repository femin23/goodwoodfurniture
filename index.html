THE ART OF WOODWORKING

Our 4-Step Handcrafted Woodworking Process

From raw timber logs to heirloom-quality furniture pieces designed for generations.

  1. 01

    Design Consultation & Floor Planning

    We analyze your architectural drawings or home layout to specify custom wood dimensions, ergonomic proportions, and wood grain matching.

  2. 02

    Seasoned Timber Log Selection

    Only fully seasoned, kiln-dried Teak and Rosewood timbers with rich organic grain patterns are selected to ensure natural termite resistance.

  3. 03

    Master Handcrafting & Joinery

    Senior craftsmen assemble every joint using traditional mortise-and-tenon woodworking techniques for superior structural strength.

  4. 04

    Multi-Stage Polishing & Delivery

    Hand-sanded to satin perfection and finished with eco-friendly oil/matte sealants before direct white-glove delivery to your doorstep in Kerala.

What types of wood are used in Good Wood Furniture collections?

We work predominantly with premium Grade-A Teak Wood (Teena/Sagwan) and natural Rosewood (Etti). Both wood species are selected for their dense grain structure, natural termite resistance, rich color, and long-term durability in tropical climates like Kerala.

  • Teak Wood: High natural oil content, golden grain, exceptional weather resilience.
  • Rosewood: Deep reddish-brown tones, supreme hardness, luxury heirloom aesthetic.
